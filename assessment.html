EXAMPLE FINDING

We tell you what matters — and why.

HIGH RISK

Administrative Access

Multiple users maintain elevated privileges beyond their day-to-day operational requirements.

Why it matters: Compromised privileged accounts can significantly increase the impact of a security incident.

Recommended action: Implement role-based administrative access, review privileged accounts regularly, and require MFA for administrative authentication.
