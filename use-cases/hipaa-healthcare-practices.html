HIPAA for Healthcare Practices

Protecting patient information takes more than good intentions.

We protect patient records, but are we overlooking compliance risks? Security and privacy guidance for doctors, dentists, and smaller healthcare providers.

Start With a $199 Security Snapshot →

The Challenge

Healthcare practices rely on staff, systems, and vendors to handle sensitive information. Unclear access, undocumented practices, and inconsistent safeguards can leave blind spots.

Why It Matters

Understanding how patient information is handled helps practices prioritize security work and prepare for a more thorough HIPAA review.

How Verdyan Helps

Start with a HIPAA Security Snapshot for a high-level review of reported safeguards and readiness gaps. Discuss formal risk analysis and broader compliance support separately.

Where We Help You Focus

  • Review access and workforce practices
  • Identify relevant systems and vendors
  • Review reported safeguards and policies
  • Prioritize follow-up work without entering patient information
