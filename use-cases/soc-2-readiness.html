SOC 2 Readiness

A clear path to meet customer, partner, and market expectations.

When a customer asks for SOC 2, it can feel overwhelming. The Verdyan™ Group helps you understand where you are, identify what’s missing, and create a practical path forward—without the confusion.

Start With a $199 Security Snapshot →

The Challenge

More customers and partners are asking for SOC 2, but many organizations aren’t sure where to start. You may have security tools in place, but lack clear documentation, defined processes, or alignment with the applicable Trust Services Criteria. This can lead to last-minute scrambles, missed opportunities, or costly delays.

Why It Matters

Customers and partners want confidence in how you protect their information. Preparing for SOC 2 helps you clarify your controls, organize evidence, and address gaps before engaging an independent auditor.

How Verdyan Helps

Our SOC 2 Security Snapshot provides a high-level review of your reported controls, policies, and practices. You’ll receive an overview of potential gaps and practical recommendations to help you plan your next steps toward readiness.

Common Areas We Examine

Policies & Procedures

Review of key policies, processes, and documentation relevant to SOC 2 readiness.

Security Controls

High-level review of reported technical and operational security practices.

Access Management

Review of user access, privileged accounts, roles, and provisioning practices.

Data Protection

Review of how data is stored, protected, monitored, and backed up.

Gaps & Next Steps

Potential gaps with practical, prioritized recommendations.

Who Is This For?

This use case is ideal for organizations that:
  • Are preparing for their first SOC 2 audit
  • Have a customer or partner requesting SOC 2
  • Want to understand their current readiness
  • Need a clear, prioritized action plan
  • Are looking to strengthen security and build customer trust
