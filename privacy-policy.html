1. Scope and contact

This Policy describes the public website of The Verdyan Group, its contact and Secure Rooms update forms, and its Security Snapshot questionnaires and payment links. Contact contact@verdyan.com with privacy questions or requests. Separate engagement agreements may address information shared during consulting. Secure Rooms is currently a development/update page; this Policy does not describe an operating customer document room.

2. Information you provide

The contact form sends your name, email, phone number if supplied, subject, and message to an Amazon Web Services (AWS) endpoint. The Secure Rooms update form sends your email and a request to join the update list to that same endpoint. Information you send by email is also available to Verdyan and its email providers. These details support responding to inquiries, arranging services, and sending the Secure Rooms updates you request. You can ask to stop those updates by emailing us.

Snapshot answers describe your organization's governance, access controls, policies, risk, operations, and security practices. Avoid including credentials, patient information, or unnecessary personal or confidential information.

3. How Snapshot data moves

  • General Security: completing the questionnaire sends its questions, categories, and answers to an AWS endpoint before checkout, even if you do not purchase. The current page sends blank name, business-name, and email fields with the assessment. It stores the returned assessment identifier and a Stripe payment URL in your browser.
  • NIST CSF: the current page calculates scores and stores answers, scores, completion time, and a generated assessment identifier in browser local storage. Its supplied page code does not send those answers to an assessment endpoint. The identifier is passed to Stripe as a checkout reference.
  • SOC 2: the current page calculates answers and readiness scores within the page and links to Stripe. Its supplied page code does not persist the answers in local storage or send them to an assessment endpoint.

Reports, related communications, and order information may contain business or contact information provided for the service. Backend storage, report preparation, and payment-to-response matching cannot be established from the public page code; see the confirmation notice below.

4. Stripe payments

Payment details are entered on Stripe-hosted checkout pages, rather than in a card-entry form on this website. Stripe processes payment and checkout information under its Privacy Policy. Verdyan may receive transaction status, purchase references, checkout contact information, and payment details made available to merchants, such as card brand and last digits; the exact checkout settings and fields available to Verdyan require confirmation. Assessment identifiers in the NIST and General Security checkout URLs help connect a payment with a Snapshot. The site's payment-success page says reports are sent to the checkout email address.

5. Browser storage, technical data, and analytics

NIST and General Security use the browser storage described above. Local storage persists beyond a page visit until cleared by you or site code; the supplied code sets no automatic expiry. On a shared device, others using the same browser profile may be able to access it. Clearing site data in your browser removes these local values and can disrupt the checkout reference. SOC 2 answers held only in page memory are lost when that page is reloaded.

No analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or site-set cookie code were identified in the supplied website files. This does not establish what the live hosting layer or Stripe uses. Hosting and service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times, browser details, and error logs to deliver and protect their services; actual logging settings require confirmation. If optional analytics are enabled later, this Policy must identify the provider, data, purpose, retention, and available choices, with consent controls where required. This wording does not authorize undisclosed tracking.

6. Access and disclosures

The observed flows involve AWS for form and General Security submissions and Stripe for checkout. Verdyan personnel and any authorized providers involved in responding to requests or delivering reports may need access to relevant information. The actual email, hosting, storage, and reporting providers and their access must be confirmed. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or as lawfully necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or legal claims. Whether information is sold, shared for targeted advertising, or used by additional providers cannot be verified from these files; no unverified promise about those practices is made here.

7. Retention and security

Browser storage behaves as described above. Server-side retention periods, backup deletion, and retention of unpaid General Security submissions are not established by the supplied files and must be confirmed. You may request deletion using the contact address below; applicable legal, accounting, or dispute obligations may affect what can be deleted. Public form and payment endpoints use HTTPS, but the website files alone do not establish encryption at rest, access controls, or other backend safeguards. No website or transfer method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Requests and choices

Email contact@verdyan.com to ask about your information, request access, correction or deletion, or stop Secure Rooms updates. Describe the request and provide the email or order reference needed to locate relevant records; do not send identification documents or sensitive information unless a suitable verification method is arranged. Rights to access, delete, correct, restrict, object, obtain a copy, withdraw consent, or complain to a regulator depend on your location and applicable law. Verdyan must evaluate requests under the laws that apply; this Policy does not claim that every regional privacy law applies or that all requests can be granted. Stripe provides separate choices for information it controls.
